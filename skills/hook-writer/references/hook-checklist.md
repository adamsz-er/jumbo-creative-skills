# Hook checklist

Pass or fail each line. A hook that fails any line is fixed or dropped.

## Video hook (seconds 0-3)

- [ ] **Seen or heard in the first 3 seconds.** The idea lands before the 3-second mark, not after a logo sting or a slow build.
- [ ] **Works with sound off.** The on-screen text and the visual carry the message on their own.
- [ ] **Names the viewer's problem or desire.** The viewer can tell what this is about, or who it is for.
- [ ] **Brand or product appears early.** Not necessarily in the first frame, but early enough that a viewer who leaves at the end of the hook still knows what was offered.
- [ ] **One idea.** A single message, not two competing ones.
- [ ] **No clickbait the ad cannot pay off.** Whatever the hook promises, the next seconds deliver.
- [ ] **Substantiable.** Every claim is one the brand can back up. Anything that needs approval is marked.
- [ ] **On brand tone.** Sounds like the brand, per the profile.
- [ ] **Readable.** On-screen text is short, high-contrast and sits inside the safe zone for the placement (check the current safe zones in the placement preview).

## Text hook (first line of primary text)

- [ ] **Stands alone.** The first sentence makes sense if the text is cut after it; check the current cut-off in the placement preview.
- [ ] **Names the viewer or their situation** before it names the product.
- [ ] **No claim the ad cannot support** and no promise of a result.
- [ ] **Matches the creative.** The words and the image or video say the same thing.

## Reading the result

A pass is not a prediction. It means the hook is clear and honest enough to be worth testing. Test one hook change at a time and judge it against the account's own ads of the same format and funnel stage.
