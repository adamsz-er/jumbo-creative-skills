# Hook archetypes

Patterns for the first moments of an ad. Each has a structure line and an example for the fictional "Acme Outdoor Co.". Choose the archetype from the viewer's starting state and awareness stage, not from what is fashionable.

| # | Archetype | Structure | Acme example |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Problem callout | Name the frustration in the viewer's words | "Your boots are soaked by lunch, and the trail is only half done." |
| 2 | Bold claim | State one strong, substantiable claim | "This shell has kept us dry on every trip this season." (only if the brand can show it) |
| 3 | Curiosity gap | Open a question the next seconds answer | "We left this pack out in the rain for a week. Watch what happened." |
| 4 | Pattern interrupt | Break the expected look or sound of a feed ad | A hand slams a boot into a bucket of water, no intro, no logo. |
| 5 | Before and after | Show the state change, then the cause | Muddy tent on the left, clean on the right: "One change." |
| 6 | POV | Put the viewer inside the moment | "POV: it is 6am, the fog has not lifted and you packed the right gear." |
| 7 | Demonstration | Do the thing, immediately, on camera | The zip is opened and closed under a running tap, in the first second. |
| 8 | Objection flip | Say the doubt out loud, then answer it | "It looks too light to be warm. Here is the test." |
| 9 | Social proof | Lead with a real customer's words or photo | A customer's trail photo with their line on screen: "Held up on day four." (illustrative: use only real reviews, with the customer's permission) |
| 10 | Us versus them | Contrast the product with the old way, not a named rival | "The old way: three layers and a prayer. The new way: one shell." |
| 11 | Founder confession | A plain admission that earns trust | "We got the first boot wrong. This is what we changed." |
| 12 | Number or list | A specific count the viewer can hold | "Three things in every day pack we send out." |
| 13 | Direct question | Ask what the viewer is facing | "Packing for your first night out and not sure what you forgot?" |
| 14 | Countdown or deadline | A real date that creates a reason to act now | "Free shipping ends Sunday." (promo only, dates from the brand) |

## Matching archetype to stage

- **Unaware:** pattern interrupt, curiosity gap, POV. They are not looking for you, so the hook has to interrupt.
- **Problem-aware:** problem callout, direct question, objection flip. Name what they already feel.
- **Solution-aware:** comparison (us versus them), demonstration, social proof. Show why this one.
- **Product-aware:** proof, founder confession, objection flip on price or fit.
- **Ready to buy:** offer, countdown, number or list of what is included.

## Notes

- Numbers, claims and dates in an archetype must come from the brand. Never invent them.
- A hook that needs the sound on is a hook for part of the audience; give it on-screen text too.
- Keep one idea per hook. A hook that tries two things stops neither.
