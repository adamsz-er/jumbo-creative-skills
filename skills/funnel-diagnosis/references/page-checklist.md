# Page checklist for a "site" call

Work through these when an ad still earns clicks but the steps after the click fall. Each is something to check, not a finding. Start with the step that fell.

## Landing-page view rate fell (clicks that never became page loads)

- Open the ad's link on a phone, on mobile data, from inside Facebook and Instagram. Does it load, and how long does it take?
- Is the link still right: the product live, not redirected to a home page or a 404, the right market and currency?
- Did anything change on the page or the site around the date the rate fell: a new theme, a pop-up, a consent banner, an app, a tracking script?
- Is the pixel or Conversions API still firing a page view? A tracking break looks like a page problem in the data. Events Manager in Meta Business Suite shows each event's activity by day.
- Did the link start opening a different destination (an in-app browser setting, a link shortener, a deep link into an app)?

## Add-to-cart rate fell (page loads that never added to cart)

- Is the product in stock in every size and colour the ad shows?
- Has the price, shipping cost or delivery time on the page changed since the ad went live? Does the page still match what the ad promises?
- Is the add-to-cart button visible without scrolling on a phone? Did a pop-up start covering it?
- Do reviews, sizing help and photos still load?

## Cart-to-checkout rate fell (carts that never started checkout)

- Add the product and go to checkout yourself, on a phone. Any error, slow step or surprise cost?
- Did shipping costs, a free-shipping threshold or taxes change?
- Did a payment option, express checkout button or discount-code field break or disappear?
- Is a discount code from the ad still valid?

## Every ad fell at once (the account-wide read says site)

Something sitewide changed: checkout, payment provider, shipping rates, a site-wide outage, stock across the range, or tracking. Check the site's own analytics for the same dates before changing any ad.

## After the check

Fix what you find, give the ads a few days of clean data, then run `keep-or-kill` again. If nothing on the page changed and the rates still fell, the audience may have shifted: compare the ad's results by placement or age with a breakdown export.
