# Creative brief template

Fill every section. Write "to confirm" rather than guess. Keep it to what a producer needs.

## Objective
One behaviour or feeling to unlock, in a sentence. (Not "increase sales": say what the viewer does or feels.)

## Persona and stage
Who it is for: their emotional starting state and awareness stage (see `persona-builder`). The funnel stage: prospecting or retargeting.

## Single message
The one thing the viewer should take away.

## Proof
What shows the message is true: a demonstration, a customer, a test, a guarantee. Every claim must be one the brand can substantiate.

## Offer and dates
The offer and its start and end dates, or "none (evergreen)". Supplied by the user.

## Ad type
bau, promo, launch, hype, partnership or retention.

## Format, placements and specs
Format (static, carousel, UGC video, founder video, creator, lo-fi or polished). Placements and aspect ratios needed: 1:1, 4:5, 9:16 as required. Safe zones: keep key text and faces inside the safe area for each placement; check the current safe zones in the placement preview. Length for video.

## Hook
- Spoken line:
- On-screen text:
- Visual action in seconds 0-3:
- Alternates (2-3, each changing one thing; see `hook-writer`):

## Script or shot list
Beat by beat: what is seen, what is said, how long. End on the brand end frame.

## On-screen text
Every line of text in the ad, in order, with where it sits.

## Visual direction
Palette, colour grading, background variety across the set, and the brand end frame.

## Casting direction
The energy and situation of the person on camera (for example, relaxed, mid-task, speaking to a friend). Never ethnicity, body type or gender assumptions.

## Tone
How it should sound and feel, per the profile. Words to avoid.

## Hero line, supporting line and CTA
- Hero line:
- Supporting line:
- CTA: a verb-first call to action ("Shop the shell", "Get the pack").

## Mandatories and must-avoids
Must show or say: logo, product, legal line, claim wording as approved. Must avoid: competitor names, unapproved claims, restricted words, anything the platform's ad policies forbid.

## Naming per deliverable
One name per file, in the naming convention: concept | format | creator | ad type | product | tone | launch date.

## How it will be judged
The funnel step this should move first (from `creative-grader`), the metrics read (for example hook rate, hold rate, CTR, CPA, ROAS, using the definitions in `creative-context/references/metrics.md`), and the comparison: the account's own median and quartiles in the same format and funnel stage, over a window the user sets. What result would count as proving the idea wrong.
