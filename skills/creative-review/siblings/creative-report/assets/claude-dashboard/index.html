        Against comparable ads

        The shaded bar is the middle half of comparable ads in this account and the tick their median. The dot is this ad: green when better than the median, red when worse, grey when neither way is better. An arrow at an end means the ad is off the scale. The verdict weighs payback and spend first, so a kept ad can still trail similar ads on a rate.
