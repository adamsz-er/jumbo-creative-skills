# Brand profile template

Save as `creative-profile.md` in the working directory. Every value comes from the user. Write `unknown` for anything they skip.

```
# Creative profile: <brand name>

Last updated: <date>

## Brand
- Name:
- Category:
- Website (optional):
- Brand tone (3 to 5 words, plus words to avoid):
- Colours and type (hex codes if known):

## Products
- Hero products or collections:
- Price range and average order value:
- Products out of stock or being phased out:

## Offers and calendar
- Standing offers (free shipping, guarantee, subscription):
- Sale and launch calendar for the next 90 days (dates and offers):
- Peak periods:

## Personas
Describe each by emotional starting state, not demographics.
- Persona 1: starts feeling ... and wants to feel ...
- Persona 2: ...

## Funnel and goals
- Primary goal (first-time buyers, repeat purchase, subscription, leads):
- Prospecting vs retargeting split:
- Markets or regions:
- Target cost per acquisition or return, if the user has one (user's own number):

## Data
- Mode: connector | csv | none
- Window and currency:
- Conversions column used: Purchases | Results | other
- Naming convention: <fields and separator> | none
- Store revenue available for MER: yes | no

## Constraints
- Claims that need approval or are not allowed:
- Usage rights and end dates for creator content:
- Approval owner:
```
