# Ad-type playbook

Six ad types, from `creative-context/references/ad-types.md`. Judge an ad against ads of its own type: a promo ad and an always-on ad do different jobs.

## bau (always-on)
- **For:** the steady baseline that runs with no end date.
- **Must carry:** a message that works without an offer, and enough variety that one concept is not carrying the account.
- **Judge:** against the account's own BAU ads in the same format. Fatigue is the main risk, so plan the next concept before the current one fades.

## promo (sale or offer)
- **For:** moving demand around a dated offer.
- **Must carry:** the offer and its dates, in the first seconds and on the end frame. Written by the user, never invented.
- **Judge:** separately from BAU. Strong payback can be existing demand being harvested, so check new-customer share before scaling. Retire the creative when the offer ends.

## launch (new product or drop)
- **For:** introducing a product or a run of new arrivals.
- **Must carry:** what is new, who it is for, and the drop cadence: fresh creative each drop rather than one ad stretched across several.
- **Judge:** against other launches, not against BAU running alongside. Do not send traffic to products that are out of stock.

## hype (teaser)
- **For:** building anticipation before a launch or sale.
- **Must carry:** a reason to care and one clear action: register, follow, or set a reminder. No price.
- **Judge:** on attention and registrations, not on revenue.

## partnership (creator or brand handle)
- **For:** borrowing a creator's audience and voice.
- **Must carry:** the usage-rights period and whether paid use is allowed, written down before the ad runs. The disclosure label the platform requires. See `creative-brief/references/creator-brief-template.md`.
- **Judge:** as a dedicated test; quality varies with the creator. Track the rights end date so the ad is switched off in time.

## retention (people who already bought)
- **For:** repeat purchase, cross-sell, loyalty.
- **Must carry:** something only a customer would care about (a refill, a pair for the product they own, early access).
- **Judge:** separately. It flatters blended results and acquires no one.

## Across all types
- One type per ad. If an ad is a launch fronted by a creator, the type is launch and the creator goes in the creator field.
- Prospecting and retargeting need different creative: attention and a clear first step for cold audiences; the specific product, proof and offer for warm ones.
- Overlay the offer and launch calendar so a spike is credited to the right type.
