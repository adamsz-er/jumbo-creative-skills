# Sale claims: hard rules

These are rules for every phase's copy, not suggestions. They follow the Australian Competition and Consumer Commission's guidance; other markets have their own regulator, and the user must check its rules before running a sale there.

Sources (the commission's own pages):

- Advertising and selling guide, two-price comparison advertising: https://www.accc.gov.au/publications/advertising-selling/advertising-and-selling-guide/pricing/two-price-comparison-advertising
- Advertising and selling guide (full guide for business, PDF): https://www.accc.gov.au/system/files/Advertising%20and%20selling%20guide%20-%20July%202021.pdf
- The commission's notice to retailers ahead of its Black Friday sweep, on time claims and countdown timers: https://www.accc.gov.au/media-release/accc-puts-retailers-on-notice-ahead-of-black-friday-sweep
- Advertising and promotions, for consumers: https://www.accc.gov.au/consumers/advertising-and-promotions

## The rules

1. **No false urgency.** "Ends today", "final hours", "3 days only" and countdown timers must match when the sale really ends. If an extension is planned, do not say the sale is ending.
2. **No resetting timers.** A countdown that restarts, or a discount that carries on after the stated end, misleads.
3. **Genuine "was" prices.** A "was" price, a struck-through price or a "save X" claim must reflect a price the product was genuinely offered at for a reasonable period before the sale. A price rarely or never charged is not a "was" price. The same goes for comparisons with a recommended retail price.
4. **"Sitewide" means sitewide.** If some products are excluded, say so plainly, or call it a category sale.
5. **"Up to" must be fair.** If the ad says "up to X% off", a reasonable part of the range has to carry that full reduction; one item at X% does not count.
6. **Stock and limits stated.** If an offer is limited (quantity, sizes, while stocks last), say so clearly. Do not advertise a price you cannot supply in reasonable quantities for a reasonable time.
7. **Gifts and conditions up front.** The rule for a gift with purchase or a tier (spend, items, dates, exclusions) appears where the offer does, not only in fine print.
8. **Chance-based offers.** Prize and chance promotions, mystery offers included, have their own rules in many places; check them before running one.

## Checking a phase's copy

For every line with a time, a price comparison or a scope ("sitewide", "everything", "all"), ask: is it true now, will it be true at the stated end, and can the brand show the evidence? If any answer is no, rewrite it. Keep the evidence (price history, the planned end date) with the brief.
