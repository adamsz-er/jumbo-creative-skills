## 1. The Weekend Doubter

- **Emotional starting state:** unsure their gear will hold up. **Wants to feel:** ready.
- **Awareness stage:** solution-aware (knows what a good boot or shell is, not yet sure Acme's holds up).
- **The job:** get through a wet weekend without the trip being spoiled by failing gear.
- **Trigger moments:** a forecast change, a trip booked, a pair of boots that leaked last time.
- **Objections:** "Will it really stay dry?" "Is it worth the price?"
- **Proof they need:** a real test, shown, not claimed. Customer photos from actual trips.
- **Language:** "held up", "soaked through", "does it actually", "not worth the risk".
- **Source:** told by the user (profile).

## 2. The Gift Buyer

- **Emotional starting state:** out of their depth. **Wants to feel:** they chose well.
- **Awareness stage:** problem-aware (knows they need a gift, not what is good).
- **The job:** pick something the recipient will actually use and not return.
- **Trigger moments:** a birthday, a holiday, a friend starting to camp.
- **Objections:** "What if it is the wrong size or the wrong thing?" "How do I know it is good?"
- **Proof they need:** simple guidance, an easy return policy, a clear "who is this for".
- **Language:** "what should I get", "something useful", "easy to return".
- **Source:** told by the user (profile). The returns line comes from the profile's standing offers (the returns policy).

## 3. The First-Night Planner

- **Emotional starting state:** overwhelmed by the list. **Wants to feel:** calm and prepared.
- **Awareness stage:** problem-aware moving to solution-aware.
- **The job:** work out exactly what to bring for a first night out.
- **Trigger moments:** booking a first campsite, a friend's invitation.
- **Objections:** "I do not want to buy things I will not use." "Where do I even start?"
- **Proof they need:** a short, honest packing list; seeing it in use by someone ordinary.
- **Language:** "first time", "what do I actually need", "beginner".
- **Source:** assumed.
