## Objective
Get a viewer who doubts their gear to watch the boot test to the end and tap through to the boot page.

## Persona and stage
The Weekend Doubter: starts unsure their gear will hold up, wants to feel ready. Solution-aware. Prospecting (cold).

## Single message
Seeing the test is better than being told.

## Proof
The boot held under running water, filmed in one continuous take. No waterproof rating is claimed (needs the founder's sign-off before any rating or warranty wording is added).

## Offer and dates
None (evergreen).

## Ad type

## Format, placements and specs
UGC video, lo-fi. Aspect ratios 9:16 and 4:5; a 1:1 cut if the placement list needs it. Keep text and faces inside the safe zone for each placement; check the current safe zones in the placement preview.

## Hook
- Spoken line: "Watch the water, not me."
- On-screen text: "Same boot. Real water."
- Visual action in seconds 0-3: the boot is held under a running tap in the first second; water beads and runs off.
- Alternates (each changes one thing): (B) water hits the boot in the first frame with no talking head; (C) a different creator delivers the same line.

## Script or shot list
1. 0-3: hook as above, one continuous take.
2. Next: the creator turns the boot to show the seam and the sole, plain language, no rating.
3. Next: one line on who it is for ("for the weekend you actually go on").
4. End: brand end frame with the boot name and the call to action.

## On-screen text
1. "Same boot. Real water." (top third, from the first frame)
2. "Built for wet weekends" (mid, during the seam shot)
3. "Shop the trail boot" (end frame)

## Visual direction
Forest green and sand from the brand palette; natural light, no filters; a workbench or trail-edge setting, kept plain; end frame in forest green with the one condensed sans-serif.

## Casting direction
The existing creator: relaxed, mid-task, talking as if to a friend. Dry humour allowed. No casting by demographic traits.

## Tone
Practical, dry humour, no hype. Avoid "epic" and "ultimate".

## Hero line, supporting line and CTA
- Hero line: Watch the water, not me.
- Supporting line: The same boot, tested on camera.
- CTA: Shop the trail boot.

## Mandatories and must-avoids
Must show: the boot, the Acme logo on the end frame. Must avoid: a waterproof rating or warranty wording (needs sign-off), competitor names, the words "epic" and "ultimate".
